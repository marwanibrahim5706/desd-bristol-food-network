Commission Report

Commission is calculated on the full order total while producer payouts remain split per producer suborder at 95%.

This report is traceable back to parent orders and producer suborders. For example, a £100 order produces £5.00 commission and £95.00 producer payout, while a £150 multi-vendor order split £80 and £70 produces £7.50 commission with payouts of £76.00 and £66.50.
